Security model

The proxy is the lock. The app still has a job.

Samsyn delegates authentication to your existing proxy. It does not delegate authorization, session security, CSRF protection, rate limits, audit, or privacy.

Trust boundary

→
2Secure proxyAuthenticates, strips, injects

3SamsynSession, authorization, CSRF

The proxy must be the only route to the app. If the app is directly reachable, anyone can forge X-User-Id.

Enforced by the app

    
Not “no security”

    
    
      
  • A server-side session bound to the proxy subject.
    • 
      
  • CSRF tokens and Origin checks on every state change.
    • 
      
  • Resource-level authorization: only participants can see a session or receipt.
    • 
      
  • Single-use, hashed join tokens; per-session verification codes.
    • 
      
  • Both participants must confirm; one-sided confirmation never verifies.
    • 
      
  • Rate limits, no-store responses, CSP, frame denial, HSTS in secure mode.
    • 
      
  • Personal numbers encrypted at rest; never logged; masked in receipts.
    • 
    

  


  

    
Required from the proxy

    
The boundary must be real

    
    
      
  • Authenticate every request, including API and static pages.
    • 
      
  • Strip any client-supplied X-User-Id or related identity headers.
    • 
      
  • Inject identity only after authentication.
    • 
      
  • Protect the proxy-to-app leg with mTLS or a shared secret.
    • 
      
  • Do not expose the app port directly to the internet.
    • 
      
  • Set X-Auth-Assurance honestly, or the app will label the identity as proxy-authenticated.
    • 
    

  






  
Residual risk

  
What this app cannot prove

  

    
Voice and video
A separate web app cannot prove the call media is unmodified. A relay or deepfake remains possible.

    
Compromised devices
Malware or a stolen session can act as a legitimate participant.

    
Group lurkers
Without call-platform integration, the app cannot see a listener who never joins the app.

    
Weak identity
A corporate directory account is not a legal identity. The assurance label must stay honest.
